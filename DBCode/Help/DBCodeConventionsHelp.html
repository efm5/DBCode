Non–Clickable Controls

The “Prefix Button” is a Button which has been “flattened” and looks like a Label. Its text always ends with “:” to give a visual indication that it is a “Prefix Button”. It and its associated control, must always share sequential TabIndexes —  with the Prefix Button’s index being the smaller.It should always be in the immediate vicinity of its associated control — typically immediately to the left, but occasionally just above.
